Capabilities

What It Does

A complete supermarket management solution with inventory tracking, cart management, and bill generation.

⊟

Inventory Viewer

Displays all products with prices, units, and stock levels in an organized view.

+

Add to Cart

Select products, enter quantities, and add them to a shopping cart in one click.

✓

Stock Validation

Prevents ordering beyond available stock with clear feedback messages.

↻

Live Stock Updates

Inventory levels update in real-time as products are added to the cart.

⊞

Bill Generation

Creates a timestamped bill with product details, quantities, and total amount.

≡

Sales History

Every transaction is logged to a persistent file for future reference.
